Privacy Policy

Last updated 14 November 2026

This page explains how VELVI NATURALS PRIVATE LIMITED handles information you choose to share through the Stevia Drop website. It is a practical summary for this site and should be read together with any notice the business gives you directly.

What this website collects

The enquiry form asks for your name, email address, phone number, and message. The site does not send that information to a server of its own and does not keep a copy of the form after you leave the page.

When you submit the form, your browser opens WhatsApp with the message filled in, so you can send it yourself. You can also choose to send the same message by email. WhatsApp and your email provider then handle the message under their own terms.

How the details are used

Details you send are used to reply to your enquiry about Stevia Drop, including availability and price. They are not sold. Please do not include information you do not want the business to see.

Links to other services

The website links to WhatsApp, email, Instagram, Amazon, and Google Maps. Those services are outside this website. If you buy through Amazon, Amazon’s own privacy terms apply to that purchase.
